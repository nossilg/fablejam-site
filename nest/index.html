For grown-ups

Fable Jam Nest is a new YouTube channel (@fablejamnest) of old fables, from Aesop and beyond, retold in our own words with Jet the DJ crow and Crumb the little mouse. Every story says its lesson out loud, the peril stays gentle, and every video ends in a good place. Season 1 starts on Thanksgiving, with a new episode every Thursday (the schedule is above).

The play corner has six little games for small hands: Launch the Airplanes, the Sound Board, Meet the Characters, Catch the Berries, Plink by Plink and Tell a Story. They collect nothing at all: no cookies, no sign-ups, no data, no ads, no links out and nothing from other companies. Leaving a game takes a grown-up's press-and-hold.

How it's made: the stories and lyrics are ours, written from public-domain translations of the old fables. The voices are made with Kokoro text-to-speech, the songs with Suno from our own lyrics, and the characters start as pictures made with Google's Gemini, then are traced into drawings our animation code can move. Soon this site will also have the old books our fables come from, and more about how we make them.
